02 / ABOUT

Engineering approach

I work at the intersection of electronics, embedded systems, sensing and control.

Instead of treating hardware and software as separate pieces, I prefer building the complete system, collecting measurements, testing assumptions and iterating from the data.

My current work includes spectral sensing, magnetic levitation, battery electronics, PCB design and digital logic.
