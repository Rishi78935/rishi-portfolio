02 / ENGINEERING PROFILE

How I work

The goal is not a pretty prototype. The goal is a system that survives contact with measurements.

I work across embedded firmware, electronics, sensors, feedback control and digital design.

My preferred workflow starts with the physical system: understand the circuit, instrument it, collect real data, test the model and then move the useful parts into firmware.

That means oscilloscopes, multimeters, serial logs, sensor data and a suspicious amount of debugging before anything deserves to be called finished.
