02 / ABOUT

Engineering approach

I work at the intersection of electronics, embedded systems, sensing and control.

I prefer complete, testable systems over isolated demonstrations: build the hardware, acquire data, test assumptions, measure the result and iterate.

Current work includes spectral sensing, magnetic levitation, battery electronics, PCB design and digital logic.
